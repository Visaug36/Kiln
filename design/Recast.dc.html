PDF to Markdown

Pull the words out of a PDF and get a Markdown file you can edit anywhere — in a text editor, a notes app, or a repository. The PDF is read by your browser and never sent anywhere.

What to expect from this pair

A PDF describes a printed page; Markdown describes structure. Some of the page cannot survive that move, so here is the honest account before you start.

Kept
Body text and paragraph order Lists and their nesting Links and their targets Bold and italic Footnote text, moved to the end
Changed
Headings are inferred from text size, since a PDF has no heading tags.
Tables become pipe tables, and merged cells are flattened into separate rows.
Images are extracted next to the file and referenced by name.
Lost
Page layout, columns and fixed positions.
Fonts, colours and anything decorative.
Wide tables past the thirteenth column.

Recast lists these again on the finished file, with the exact count from your document.

▶ 2 notes about scanned PDFs and encryption
A scanned PDF is a picture of a page. Recast has no text recognition, so it will tell you the file is an image and stop rather than guess.
Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked in the tool that made them first.

Why is this page not a server?

Converters that upload your file keep a copy, however briefly. A lease, a payslip or a contract is nobody else's business — so Recast does the work here instead.

The icon set — 14 formats at 64px and 20px

One tile, one letterform, fourteen colours

Same square, same radius, same weight — only the letters and the colour change, so the colour does the identifying before anyone reads the label. Hues are picked by association with each format's own software, not copied from it. The three OpenDocument formats carry the Writer, Calc and Impress letter over a baseline bar, which keeps them related to their counterparts and distinct from them.

App icon — the staff · 16 / 32 / 64 / 512
512

A staff, drawn as an instrument

Recast, as in recasting a spell. The mark is a staff: a shaft with a cut stone at the head. Two primitives, no ornament — the idea carries the joke, the drawing stays as precise as the rest of the interface.

Two elements, because 16px is the constraint that decides everything: a 24-unit cut stone mounted on a 34-unit shaft, 7 units wide, ending square like a rod. At 16px that is a 6px diamond over a 1.75px stem — the head is wide enough for its facets to survive rasterisation, and there is no third part small enough to fuse with anything. A ring would fill in at that size and a circle would read as a map pin.
